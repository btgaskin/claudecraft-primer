# Sources, evidence, and adaptation

## Starting point

US Government. *A Tradecraft Primer: Structured Analytic Techniques for Improving Intelligence Analysis*. March 2009. Hosted by the Central Intelligence Agency's Center for the Study of Intelligence.

- [Official publication page](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/books-monographs/a-tradecraft-primer)
- [Official PDF](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Tradecraft-Primer-apr09.pdf)

The skill adapts the primer's twelve techniques through original procedural summaries. The references cite printed page numbers. The original historical examples and quoted third-party passages are not reproduced. The primer's discussion of selecting techniques across a project, pp. 38–39, informs the skill's selective use of methods.

## Empirical findings that shape this adaptation

**Dhami, M. K., Belton, I. K., & Mandel, D. R. (2019).** “The ‘analysis of competing hypotheses’ in intelligence analysis.” *Applied Cognitive Psychology*, 33, 1080–1090. [doi:10.1002/acp.3550](https://doi.org/10.1002/acp.3550).

Fifty intelligence analysts were randomly assigned to use ACH or not in a hypothesis-testing task with probabilistic ground truth. The authors found incomplete adherence, mixed evidence of reduced confirmation bias, and indications that ACH could increase inconsistency and error. These results concern the tested task and implementation. They motivate checking the outcome of an analytical procedure, including whether its steps are usable, instead of treating procedure completion as a quality guarantee.

**Karvetski, C. W., & Mandel, D. R. (2020).** “Coherence of probability judgments from uncertain evidence: Does ACH help?” *Judgment and Decision Making*, 15(6), 939–958. [Article](https://sjdm.org/~baron/journal/20/200430/jdm200430.html).

In an experiment with 227 participants, evidence varied in source reliability and information credibility. ACH showed no advantage over a no-technique control on multiple coherence tests. The ACH group also showed pseudo-diagnostic weighting. This motivates preserving source quality and evidential dependence explicitly and avoiding numerical certainty produced by a scoring rubric.

**Dhami, M. K., Belton, I. K., De Werd, P., Hadzhieva, V., & Wicke, L. (2024).** “Effects of task structure and confirmation bias in alternative hypotheses evaluation.” *Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications*, 9, 37. [doi:10.1186/s41235-024-00560-y](https://doi.org/10.1186/s41235-024-00560-y).

Study 1 (161 participants) examined information layout: hypotheses in rows performed better on the tested confirmation-bias measure, while the conventional ACH orientation did not confer that benefit or increased sensitivity to evidence credibility. Study 2 found that most of 62 Dutch military analysts did not exhibit the tested confirmation bias and were sensitive to credibility. This research examined particular task features and behaviors rather than the effectiveness of the complete ACH procedure. It motivates evaluating presentation and avoiding a blanket assumption that analysts require correction for the same bias.

## Design choices

- Retain explicit assumptions, source review, comparison of alternatives, and revision conditions as useful ways to organize an assessment.
- Omit automatic ACH inconsistency scores and indicator-count rankings. Consider supporting and conflicting evidence, prior information, dependence, and observability.
- Select methods and effort according to the question. Keep answer length separately adjustable.
- Label role-based accounts and scenarios; preserve material uncertainty in first-person sketches.
- Treat AI-generated perspectives as constructed alternatives, with no claim of independent corroboration.

These are design choices informed by the primer and the studies, not experimentally established improvements in AI reasoning. Removing scoring does not establish that the remaining structure improves accuracy. Practical evaluation should examine the quality and usefulness of the resulting assessment, including cases where a light treatment is enough.
